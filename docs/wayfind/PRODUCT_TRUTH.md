## Place-card hook — the house bar (owner, 2026-08-20)

### The two-beat rule

One sourced distinction (award / named plate / craft) **plus** one physical
why-sit (patio, dock, room, sand, pond). Short. Complete.

be sourced, the slot is empty. Do not invent replacement copy.
