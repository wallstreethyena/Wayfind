# TikTok creator intake, 2026-10-08

Owner request (2026-10-08): add 12 TikTok links (11 unique; one was sent twice)
to the matching place cards.

## How each link was verified

- Short links resolved to their canonical `tiktok.com/@<handle>/video/<id>` URL.
- Both creators gate their videos behind TikTok's "audience controls", so the
  caption and place tag of every video were read in the signed-in TikTok
  browser (the owner signed in himself; no credentials were handled).
- Each venue was then opened on its own Google Maps listing to confirm name,
  address and that it is open. The `placeId` on each entry is that listing's
  own Google id. The conversion from the Maps listing to the id was checked
  against three ids already stored in `wf_inventory` (Lucky Tigre, Mi Tierra
  Latina, Fat Beet Farm) and against `wf_place_ids` for Sweetfields Farm; all
  four matched exactly.
- Captions are Wayfind's own words and claim only what the video and the
  listing support. No prices, dates or ratings are repeated.
