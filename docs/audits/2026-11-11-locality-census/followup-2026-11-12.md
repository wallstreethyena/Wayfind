The place-insert path (`lib/seedPlaces.js` `buildInventoryRow`, reached through the
`promote-index` and `scout` jobs) writes no city or county. Every place added after the
2026-11-11 backfill therefore arrived with neither: all 30 rows added 11-11 → 11-12.

that contain the row's own point; add-only, each write conditional on city AND county both
still empty; a 5-row batch first, checked on the live page, then the other 25.
