## Reproduced discrepancies against live Wayfind rows

- `/tmp/fall-live.json` was a successfully read live Fall API response with ten rails and real card rows. This was a read-only comparison, not a production data write.
- Pumpkin Parlour's source proves Sunday closes 4pm. An open-ended season is not evidence it is open every evening.
- Sweetfields stored $11.95 excludes $1.33 mandatory service fee; stored schedule omits Friday Oct 2. Ticket arrival is date-specific, not a timed slot or online-only gate.
- Fruitville still advertised free admission/$5 parking. Current organizer changed the pricing to $5 admission/free parking.
- Scream stored $17–$22 parking and mostly midnight closes. Current standard parking is $19, preferred $24 optional; dated Hours source has real 11pm/1am sessions.
- HHN stored 48 nights and omitted Monday Oct 12. Current published weekly rule derives 49 regular dates. Do not turn under13 warning into hard minimum.
- LEGOLAND stored Oct31 close/older 18-date list. Current page adds future Nov1 and explicitly advertises after-dark experiences. Earlier official blogs remain contradictory.
- Hyde Park's generic header conflicts with Mon–Thu body hours; use the explicit weekday schedule.

## Deliberate unknowns and follow-up

- Pumpkin Parlour has no published closing date. Its open-ended weekly schedule needs refreshing when organizer changes the season; no invented end date was added.
- Full-event clocks are unknown for LEGOLAND, SeaWorld and Hollowgraves. Candy-station, park and host-festival windows must not prove continuous event operation. HHN closing is also unknown.
- The registry preserves all dates explicitly published across LEGOLAND's conflicting sources, with no full-event hours or `dates_only` claim. Future dates from the current page are strong; historical completeness remains unresolved.
- SeaWorld October dates were visibly checked in the cloud browser after the public web calendar omitted its dynamic cells. Historic season dates are left unchanged rather than claimed newly verified. SeaWorld's current product ($37) supersedes stale FAQ ($35), but date/checkout price must be reconfirmed.
- Pumpkin Parlour/Hyde Park explicit admission charges, Hollowgraves charge and exact current HHN ticket price remain unknown. Scream prices are dynamic and its observed lower bound is dated evidence, not a permanent price promise.
- No ticket URL or `minimum_age` was changed; advisories are stored as readable restrictions, distinct from genuine 18+ interactive opt-in.
