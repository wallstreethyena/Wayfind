# Flagship parks verified-line pack

Research date: 2026-10-31. Published 2026-10-31 with the canonical guarded
publisher (`scripts/publish-owned-editorial.mjs --commit`), reviewer
"Gabriel (owner) via Muse research session 2026-10-31".

## Why

The flagship theme-park cards (Florida's Biggest Parks rail) carried no
verified editorial line for 9 of 17 parks, even though cards switched to
verified-only lines in #2087. This pack closes that gap with researched,
source-cited rows: `hook` (known for), `why_here` (why go), `know_before`,
and `local_tip` (what to try) per park.

## Sources

Official venue pages (universalorlando.com, legoland.com,
peppapigthemepark.com) and Visit Orlando park guides (tourism DMO) only.
disney.com is a blocked host under the publisher rules, so the four Disney
parks cite Visit Orlando. Each source was read on the research date; claims
are concise factual paraphrases, and every prose field cites its evidence.

## Result

9/9 rows written as verified `wf_editorial` rows (0 placeholder
replacements). Live check after publish: all 17 flagship parks resolve a
verified ("wayfind" tier) line via `/api/known-for`.
