# Resale tickets

The 2026-09-23 decision declining resale tickets is recorded in
`lib/eventTicketDeals.js` on `NO_EXACT_AFFILIATE_PRODUCT["rock-the-universe-2027"]`
(PR #1468). Universal sells Rock the Universe on its own store. The owner
decision that day was to keep that button hidden and not use a resale listing
just to create a monetized button.

## 2026-10-23

Founder (Gabe) reversed the 2026-09-23 no on resale tickets and approved the
TicketNetwork resale offer for World Series Game 3
(`stpete-event-ws-game-3-2026`, PR #1897). Affiliate disclosure on that offer
is unchanged. The Rock the Universe 2027 no-exact-product row is unchanged.
