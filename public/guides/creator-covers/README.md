# Disney holiday food creator cover

`santas-holiday-grove-chocolate-tree-croissant.webp` shows the Chocolate Tree Croissant served at Santa’s Holiday Grove. Disney Parks Blog published the photo in its official 2026 menu. Wayfind cropped it to 3:4, resized it to 660 × 880, and converted it to WebP. Credit goes to Walt Disney World.
